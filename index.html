Section 2.5 A

Definition, Addition & Subtraction of Complex Numbers

A complex number has the standard form \(a + bi\) where \(a\) is the Real Part and \(b\) is the Imaginary Part.

Standard Form
a + bi
Real Part \(a\) A real number (e.g. \(3, -1, 0, \sqrt{2}\))
Imaginary Part \(b\) Coefficient of \(i\) (Note: \(b\) is a real number!)

Senior Level Challenges

Challenging Exam-Style Problems & Guided Walkthroughs

Based directly on textbook Examples 2.20, 2.21, and 2.22. Master algebraic parameter solving with complex numbers!

Challenge 1 (Textbook Ex 2.20) Level 2 Algebraic Parameter
Let \(k\) be a real number. Simplify \(8 - \frac{k + 8i}{i}\) and express the result in the form \(a + bi\).

Detailed Step-by-Step Walkthrough:

Step 1: Multiply numerator and denominator of the fraction by \(i\) to rationalize:

\(8 - \frac{k + 8i}{i} \times \frac{i}{i} = 8 - \frac{ki + 8i^2}{i^2}\)

Step 2: Substitute \(i^2 = -1\):

\(= 8 - \frac{ki + 8(-1)}{-1} = 8 - \frac{ki - 8}{-1}\)

Step 3: Divide by \(-1\):

\(= 8 - (-(ki - 8)) = 8 + (ki - 8)\)

Step 4: Simplify constants:

\(= ki\)

Challenge 2 (Textbook Ex 2.21) Power Cycling + Division
Find the real part of \(\frac{i^{10} + 2i^9}{1 - i}\).

Detailed Step-by-Step Walkthrough:

Step 1: Simplify powers of \(i\) using remainders when divided by 4:

\(10 \div 4 = 2 \text{ R } 2 \implies i^{10} = i^2 = -1\)

\(9 \div 4 = 2 \text{ R } 1 \implies i^9 = i^1 = i\)

The fraction becomes \(\frac{-1 + 2i}{1 - i}\)

Step 2: Rationalize by multiplying by conjugate \((1 + i)\):

\(\frac{(-1 + 2i)(1 + i)}{(1 - i)(1 + i)} = \frac{-1 - i + 2i + 2i^2}{1^2 - i^2} = \frac{-1 + i - 2}{1 - (-1)} = \frac{-3 + i}{2}\)

Step 3: Separate into \(a + bi\):

\(= -\frac{3}{2} + \frac{1}{2}i\)

Step 4: Extract Real Part:

\(\text{Real Part} = -\frac{3}{2}\)

Challenge 3 (Textbook Ex 2.22) Purely Real Condition
If \(k\) and \(\frac{10}{3 + i} - ki\) are real numbers, find the value of \(k\).

Detailed Step-by-Step Walkthrough:

Step 1: Simplify \(\frac{10}{3 + i}\) by multiplying top and bottom by conjugate \((3 - i)\):

\(\frac{10(3 - i)}{(3 + i)(3 - i)} = \frac{30 - 10i}{3^2 - i^2} = \frac{30 - 10i}{9 - (-1)} = \frac{30 - 10i}{10} = 3 - i\)

Step 2: Substitute back into original expression:

\(3 - i - ki = 3 - (1 + k)i\)

Step 3: Since the expression is a REAL number, its imaginary part MUST be 0:

\(-(1 + k) = 0 \implies 1 + k = 0\)

\(k = -1\)
